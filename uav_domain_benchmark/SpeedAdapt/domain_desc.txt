This domain models an autonomous uav navigating through three types of terrain-cluttered areas, narrow gaps, and open spaces-where adaptive speed control is essential for safe traversal. The uav moves between connected positions and can dynamically adjust its speed by accelerating rate or decelerating rate. Each terrain type imposes specific speed constraints: In cluttered and narrow regions, the uav must slow down to a speed below the terrain's maximum allowable speed. In open areas, it must speed up to a value above the terrain's minimum speed threshold. A position becomes passable once the uav's current speed satisfies the constraints of the terrain it is entering.
