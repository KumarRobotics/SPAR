In this domain, a uav (unmanned aerial vehicles) is tasked with identifying and grasping good fruits by clamping its gripper at different locations. Each uav must first move to the location of a fruit, capture an image to assess whether the fruit is good or not, if the fruit is not rotten, then uav will mark the fruit as good fruit, otherwise it will mark it as "not good". The uav only grasp fruits that are marked as good. uavs can transport fruits to new locations, and only take one fruit once at a time. The uav can move between two locations and this will cost specific energy between two locations, it must recharg to full capacity at the specific charging spots when needed.
