In this domain, a uav operates in an environment where it must navigate complex spatial constraints and interact with objects through manipulation and transport. One of the uav's key capabilities is the ability to dynamically shrink its body, allowing it to pass through gaps that would otherwise be impassable. This shrinking mechanism also plays a critical role during object manipulation: the uav must shrink its shape to grasp and transport packages. The uav is able to detect whether a location is a gap, and can decide whether to pass through, grasp, or release based on its physical configuration and situational awareness.
