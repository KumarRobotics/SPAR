This domain models a logistics environment, where both uavs and trucks are used to transport packages between different locations across multiple cities. Each location belongs to a city, and some locations are airports. Trucks operate within a single city, driving between locations in the same city, while uavs are restricted to flying only between airports in different cities. Packages can be located at a specific location, inside a truck, or inside a uav. Trucks and uavs can load and unload packages when they are at the same location as the package. However, uavs can only perform loading and unloading at airport locations.
