This domain models a UAV-based passenger transport system in a structured environment with multiple locations. The UAV is responsible for transporting passengers between locations while considering battery capacity and payload limits. The uav can move between locations if it has enough battery to cover the energy cost of the trip. It can also board a passenger at a location if the passenger’s weight does not exceed the UAV’s remaining load capacity. Passengers can deboard at the UAV's current location, and the UAV can recharge its battery to its full capacity at designated charging stations.
