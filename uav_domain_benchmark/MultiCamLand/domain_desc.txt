In this domain, a uav operates in a 2D grid environment populated with obstacles. The uav is equipped with two cameras: a side-mounted directional camera that can observe cells to its immediate left or right (depending on its orientation), and a downward-facing camera that detects the cell directly beneath it. The uav can rotate in place to change its viewing direction for side detection, and it uses the bottom camera to assess the safety of the cell below. A position is marked as clear if no obstacle is detected. The uav's objective is to safely navigate the environment by identifying obstacle-free cells and avoiding hazards. It can move in the four cardinal directions (up, down, left, right), but only to positions confirmed to be safe. Each action—whether movement, rotation, or detection—incurs a cost.
