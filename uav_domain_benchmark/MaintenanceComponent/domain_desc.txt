In this domain, a uav is used to inspect and assess the condition of components. The uav can perform two tasks: 1. take images of components and analysis them; 2. measuring thermal value of components and analysis them. The uav must only perform only one task at a time, that means it will not be avaiable before it finishes the data analysis in each task. A component may be identified as anomalous if it has broken when analyzing the image or has over-heat thermal value when analyzing the thermal value.
