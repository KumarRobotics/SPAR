Example 6. ugv pushs the movable obstacles to another position
Action: This action enables the ugv pushes a movable obstacle from an adjacent position to another new position that is adjacent to that obstacle's location, given that the new position is marked as clear and not equal to the ugv's current location. For example, if the ugv is at Position_1, and Position_2 is marked as having-moveable-obstacles, the ugv can push the obstacle to Position_3 and move to Position_2 if Position_3 is marked as clear, connected to Position_2 and not equal to Position_1.
