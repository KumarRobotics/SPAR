This domain simulates a mixed robot team composed of uavs and ugvs operating in an environment. The environment is discretized into connected positions. Each position may be free of obstacles, or may has fixed obstacle, or movable obstacle. uavs can fly between positions and detect if there are obstacles and if the obstacles are fixed or movable. ugvs, on the other hand, can only move between clear positions and are capable of physically pushing movable obstacles to adjacent positions to clear a path.
