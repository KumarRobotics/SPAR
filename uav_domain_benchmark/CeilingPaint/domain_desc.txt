This domain models an ceiling-painting task performed by a uav (unmanned aerial vehicle). The uav can take off, fly between positions, and land. It can pick up or put down a sprayer, and use it to paint ceiling tiles at different locations. Each sprayer is preloaded with a specific color. The uav must be flying and located at a target position in order to paint it. Each location can only be painted once and can only be painted with specific color. All actions incur a numeric cost.
