The warehouse contains three packages: package1, package2, and package3. Each package must be delivered to its designated destination: package1 to location1, package2 to location2, and package3 to location3. The UAV starts at the warehouse, and returns to the warehouse upon completing all deliveries.
The UAV capacity is 10 units. The weights of package1, package2, and package3 are 2 units, 9 units, 7 units.
Distance from warehouse to location1 is 3 units and the same in reverse direction.
Distance from warehouse to location2 is 2 units and the same in reverse direction.
Distance from warehouse to location3 is 5 units and the same in reverse direction.
Distance from location1 to location2 is 6 units and the same in reverse direction.
Distance from location1 to location3 is 1 units and the same in reverse direction.
Distance from location2 to location3 is 2 units and the same in reverse direction.
