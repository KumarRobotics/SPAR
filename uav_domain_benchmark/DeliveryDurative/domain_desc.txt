This domain models a uav-based delivery system where uavs transport packages between locations and deliver them into warehouses. A warehouse is also a location and packages can only be deliverd into warehouse locations. Note that packages can not be picked up at a warehouse. Each uav can carry only one package at a time and must hold nothing before picking up another. Each Package has associated value, total value accumulates after packages are delivered to warehouse locations. Each movement between two locations incurs a specific time cost, and the total travel duration is also accumulated when moving between locataions.
