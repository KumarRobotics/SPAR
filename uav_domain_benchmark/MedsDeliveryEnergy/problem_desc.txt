There is one central hospital and five other hospitals: Hospital1, Hospital2, Hospital3, Hospital4 and Hospital5. The UAV must deliver blood sample Sample1 to Hospital1, Sample2 to Hospital2, Sample3 to Hospital3, Sample4 to Hospital4, and Sample5 to Hospital5. 
All samples are initially located at the central hospital.
The totol energy power of battery is 10 units, there are recharging station at central hospital, Hospital2, Hospital5, which can recharge 8, 6, 5 units of power.
Travel from central hospital to Hospital1 will cost 4 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from central hospital to Hospital2 will cost 3 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from central hospital to Hospital3 will cost 4 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from central hospital to Hospital4 will cost 5 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from central hospital to Hospital5 will cost 5 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from Hospital1 to Hospital2 will cost 1 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from Hospital1 to Hospital3 will cost 3 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from Hospital1 to Hospital4 will cost 3 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from Hospital1 to Hospital5 will cost 4 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from Hospital2 to Hospital3 will cost 2 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from Hospital2 to Hospital3 will cost 2 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from Hospital2 to Hospital4 will cost 4 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from Hospital3 to Hospital4 will cost 6 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from Hospital3 to Hospital5 will cost 3 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
Travel from Hospital4 to Hospital5 will cost 4 units of power and the same in reverse direction.
The goal is to find the most efficient delivery route that minimizes total battery consumption while ensuring all blood samples are successfully delivered.
