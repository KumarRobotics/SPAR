In this domain, an unmanned aerial vehicle (uav) is responsible for delivering blood samples between various locations. Each uav has a limited battery, and travel from one location to another consumes energy. uavs must keep their battery level positive. uavs can only move to new locations if they have sufficient battery, and reassign its battery at specific locations depending on reassign capacity of the locations. A uav can pick up and carry one sample at a time, it can also unload the sample at some location.
